Error: This data has two waves (1, 2) and the formula has no lagged relation term, so both waves would supply an outcome. Fitting them together would treat two observations of the same actors as independent given the AME structure, which they are not, and this package does not do that. Either add prior_tie() -- which makes the earlier wave the initial condition and models the transition -- or name what to fit with waves = c(1, 2), which fits the later wave and leaves the earlier one available to lagged predictors.
