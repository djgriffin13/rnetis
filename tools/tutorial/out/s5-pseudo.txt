Pseudo R-squared (selection model, group team)
------------------------------------------------------------
  Latent-scale variance share (fixed effects)       0.285  [0.227, 0.339]
  Tjur's D (probability scale)                      0.253
  Null-comparison pseudo R2 (latent scale)         -0.013

  The latent-scale variance share is this package's own
  construction, not a published statistic.

  The null comparison on a binary probit: the residual variance is
  fixed at 1 for identification, so predictors cannot reduce it and
  the reduction can appear only in the additive components. It
  therefore understates explained variation relative to a
  linear-scale analogue, and is not comparable with a linear-model
  R-squared.

  The null comparison is negative. It is deliberately not clipped
  to zero: the full model did not reduce the unexplained latent
  variance relative to the null. Because the two are separate MCMC
  fits, a small negative value can also be Monte Carlo error; a
  longer chain distinguishes the two.

Different measures answer different questions; none is 'the' R-squared.
