Error: vcov = "cluster" with by = "group" needs more than one group to cluster on, and this fit has one. A clustered sandwich estimates its outer variance from variation ACROSS clusters, so a single cluster supplies one term and no estimate. No variance estimator in rnetis models residual dependence inside a SINGLE network, so rnetis reports that it cannot compute this rather than returning a number from a different model. Do NOT substitute by = "node": clustering on actors assumes actors are independent clusters, which is precisely what residual network dependence violates, so it answers a different question rather than a weaker version of this one.
